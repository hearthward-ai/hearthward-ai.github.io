What this is

Hearthward is a free assistant that runs on your own computer with a local, open-weight language model. It is meant to keep an eye on your calendar, messages, email and deadlines, and to remind you before something slips instead of waiting to be asked. It is free to use and runs on a computer you already own: a capable PC with a modern graphics card (exact requirements will come with the release). Nothing leaves your computer unless a feature needs it.

It is built on the open-source Hermes Agent (Nous Research).

It is a personal engineering project by Tony, a university student. It is not a product or a company, and nobody can sign up for it yet.

A guard for outside text

An assistant that reads web pages, emails and messages can be tricked by instructions hidden in them (this is called prompt injection). Hearthward includes a guard that blocks harmful instructions hidden in that text. It is a supporting feature, built to make the assistant safe enough to leave running. It has three parts:

Threshold

Outside text is scanned for hidden text and injection tricks, then fenced off so the model is told to treat it as data, not as orders (best effort).

Knock

Any action on your behalf asks first. The model only writes text; sending and changing things happen in fixed code that waits for your yes.

Gatehouse

Replies are checked on the way out, and only approved recipients can be messaged. If the guard can't prove it is active, the assistant stays offline.

For you, or an add-on for your family

Hearthward is mainly a personal tool. If you want, it can also be set up to help family members at home, each with their own limits on what it may do and whom it may contact.

Why I am building it

I wanted a 24/7 assistant for my own student life: tracking schedules and deadlines, planning my workload across the week and drafting emails. Most AI helpers that can do this cost a monthly fee and send everything to someone else's servers. I wanted something free and private that I, and people close to me, could trust with daily tasks.

Status

Hearthward is in early development and is not released. Parts of it are being built and tested privately. There is no download yet. For how data is handled, see the privacy policy.
