Privacy policy

Hearthward (Gmail connection). Last updated: 2026-11-16.

Home Google user data Deletion Contact

In short

  • The Gmail connection can only send email you have approved. It cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • The permission token stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is kept on a Hearthward server.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared or used to train AI models. You can remove access at any time.

Who this covers

Hearthward is in early development. The assistant, including the Gmail connection, is not publicly released; only the guard code is public, as an early, best-effort release. This policy describes how the Gmail connection is built and tested today, and how it will work when released.

Operator: Tony (GitHub: TonyC115), for the Hearthward project. The Google app is named exactly “Hearthward”.

Hearthward is a private assistant that its owner runs on their own home computer, for themselves and, if they choose, their family. It is not a commercial service, it has no public sign-up, and it shows no ads. This policy explains what happens to data, and in particular to Google user data, when a Gmail account is connected to the Hearthward Google app.

Google user data the app can access

When the account owner connects a Gmail account, the Hearthward Google app asks Google for exactly these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send: send email on the account's behalf.
  • openid and email: see the account's email address.

With these permissions Hearthward cannot read, search, label, change or delete any email in the account, and it has no access to contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

  • Email address: checked once, when the account is connected, to confirm that the intended account gave permission.
  • Sending: the send permission is used only to send email that its owner has approved, from the connected account to recipients the owner has allowed. The language model cannot send email or choose a recipient on its own.
  • Email content: messages are written on the home computer by a local language model. Their content is not sent to any outside AI service.

What is stored, and where

  • The permission token from Google, the connected email address and the list of granted permissions are stored locally in a private file on the owner's own computer, with owner-only access.
  • A local technical log records the time of each send, the Gmail message id and the number of recipients. It never contains email content, and older entries are overwritten automatically.
  • Sent emails are kept by Gmail in the account's own Sent folder, under Google's privacy policy. Hearthward keeps no copy on any server.

How long data is kept

  • The permission token is kept until the owner disconnects the app (see below), which deletes it.
  • Log entries are kept only until the size-limited log overwrites them. No fixed number of days has been set.

Sharing

Hearthward does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model. No person reads it, except the owner when fixing a technical problem.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

All traffic to Google is encrypted (HTTPS). The connection step uses Google's standard sign-in with a one-time code; Hearthward never sees the account's credentials. The permission token stays on the home computer.

Removing access and deleting data

  • The account owner can remove access at any time in the Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
  • The owner can also run Hearthward's disconnect command, which revokes the permission at Google and deletes the stored token from the computer.
  • Log entries are overwritten automatically as the size-limited log rotates. To have them deleted sooner, contact the operator.

Children

Hearthward is not directed at children and is used only by adults the operator knows personally.

Changes

If the app ever asks for a different Google permission, this policy will be updated first and the date at the top will change. Reading email is planned; it would need a different permission, and this policy would be updated first.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact TonyC115 through github.com/hearthward-ai. Open a short issue asking for a private way to reach me, and do not put personal details in it. I aim to reply within about a week; this is a personal engineering project, not a staffed service.

Security problems in the guard code: please do not post details publicly. Use ‘Report a vulnerability’ on the Security tab of the guard repository (see its SECURITY.md). Security problems with this website: please do not post details publicly. Open a short issue titled ‘Security report, please contact me privately’ at the repository’s issues page with no details, and I will reply with a private channel. If the repository’s Security tab shows ‘Report a vulnerability’, you can use that instead.
