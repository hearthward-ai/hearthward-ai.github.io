Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

No dates are promised, and the order may change. This is a personal engineering project that moves around my studies, so progress comes in bursts. Status words:

  • Done: built and working, as far as tested so far.
  • In progress: being built or tested privately.
  • Planned: not started.
  • Blocked: waiting on something outside my control.

Done so far

  • A first working version of the guard with automated tests and an attack test kit, published as an early, best-effort release: hearthward-guard (Apache-2.0)
  • A Gmail connection that can only send email you have approved, built and tested privately
  • This website, in English and Simplified Chinese, with a privacy policy

In progress

  • Improving and testing the guard: Threshold, Knock and Gatehouse
  • Chatting with the assistant in a chat app (Discord first)
  • Reminders, deadlines and a daily brief

Planned

  • A workload planner that spreads tasks across the week
  • A public release of the rest of the assistant, once a complete, tested version is ready
  • Setup notes and PC requirements to go with that release

Blocked

  • Reading your email inbox through the guard: this needs Google’s permission to read email, so it is not done yet
