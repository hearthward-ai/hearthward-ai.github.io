What the guard can and cannot do

The guard (Threshold, Knock and Gatehouse) is designed to make an assistant safer to leave running. It reduces the risk of prompt injection; it does not eliminate it. A new or well-disguised attack may get through, and a check can also hold back text that was harmless. The guard is early and still in development. This site describes the intended design; the published code (hearthward-guard) is the current early version and may differ.

Do not rely on it as your only protection. Keep anything that matters (accounts, money, private files) behind your own safeguards, and read what the assistant asks to do before you approve it.
